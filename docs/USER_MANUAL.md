# StayJi User Manual

## Dashboard

Use the user dashboard to track wishlisted stays, visit interest, viewed properties, comparisons, notifications, and StayJi Coins.

## Finding Stays

Search by city, locality, budget, sharing, amenities, food, AC/non-AC, and availability. Demo listings are marked as Demo Property, Sample Listing, or Coming Soon Area until a city is fully launched.

## Visits And Move-In Proof

After you join a property, open the property page and submit move-in proof with owner name, joining date, payment screenshot, and optional room image. StayJi reviews the proof only after the owner confirms that you joined successfully.

## StayJi Coins

The wallet shows total coins, approved rewards, pending rewards, and reward history. Coins are approved only after payment proof, property verification, move-in confirmation, and owner confirmation.

## Reviews

You can rate and review properties. Reviews update the property average rating. Owners can reply, and admins can moderate reviews that are abusive, fake, or unsafe.
